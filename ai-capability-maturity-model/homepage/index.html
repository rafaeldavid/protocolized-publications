The AI Capability Maturity Model reframes AI adoption as a protocol design problem. Shadow adoption, quality risk, trust collapse, and over-reliance are all protocol failures — breakdowns in the rules governing how humans and AI systems interact. A maturity model built on that framing generates different, more specific recommendations than one organized around security controls or tooling adoption rates. Built for deployment managers and operations executives.

The Five Levels

From shadow to planetary

  • L1 Shadow

    Individuals adopt AI tools without organizational sanctioning. Adoption is real; visibility, policy, and review are not yet in place.

    Like spreadsheets at every desk before IT had a policy.

  • L2 Sanctioned

    AI access has been granted top-down. The work of designing what AI does and how it is reviewed has not yet happened.

    Like Bill Gates's 1995 Internet Tidal Wave memo to Microsoft executives.

  • L3 Designed

    Workflows are AI-native by design with named owners, review protocols, and quality measurement.

    Like the Git pull request — coordination absorbed into the artifact itself.

  • L4 Infrastructural

    AI is infrastructural across the industry — other systems and partners depend on it. The mandate and the operational receiving end are paired.

    Like Walmart's 1988 EDI mandate that elevated all of US retail.

  • L5 Planetary

    AI as ambient coordination substrate. The protocol is invisible to most participants — until a chokepoint event makes it visible.

    Like the global container shipping protocol since 1956.

About

The Protocol Institute

The Protocol Institute is an independent research organization studying protocols — the rules and coordination structures that shape interaction across diplomacy, software, medicine, governance, and beyond. Evolved from the Ethereum Foundation-funded Summer of Protocols program (2023–2025), it continues that work through research, publishing, and community building across organizational theory, infrastructure studies, and governance design. Protocolized is its flagship publication; the AI Capability Maturity Model is one of its practitioner-facing frameworks, produced by the Protocols for Business leads.
