The AI Capability Maturity Model reframes AI adoption as a protocol design problem. Shadow adoption, quality risk, trust collapse, and over-reliance are all protocol failures — breakdowns in the rules governing how humans and AI systems interact. A maturity model built on that framing generates different, more specific recommendations than one organized around security controls or tooling adoption rates. Built for deployment managers and operations executives.

About

The Protocol Institute

The Protocol Institute is an independent research organization studying protocols — the rules and coordination structures that shape interaction across diplomacy, software, medicine, governance, and beyond. Evolved from the Ethereum Foundation-funded Summer of Protocols program (2023–2025), it continues that work through research, publishing, and community building across organizational theory, infrastructure studies, and governance design. Protocolized is its flagship publication; the AI Capability Maturity Model is one of its practitioner-facing frameworks, produced by the Protocols for Business Special Interest Group.

Rafael Fernandez
Protocols for Business SIG

Rafael investigates how protocols — from cryptographic systems to organizational procedures — enable coordination in autonomous enterprises and AI-augmented management. His work explores the intersection of protocol design, technology adoption, and firm structure, arguing that durable value emerges where coordination becomes structurally unavoidable during deployment rather than during speculative installation phases.

Sachin Benny
Protocols for Business SIG

Sachin examines how technology reshapes culture and civilization, exploring temporal shifts in culture and technology through lenses of memory, knowledge systems, and artificial intelligence. His research investigates the intersection of protocols, general-purpose technologies, and social acceleration — analyzing how tools like LLMs and technological infrastructure transform human behavior and organizational dynamics.
