# Vibe Coding and the Maker Movement (Technically)

## Summary
Argues that "vibe coding" — building software by prompting LLMs without close inspection of what's produced — skipped the traditional *scenius* phase that earlier maker movements went through, in which hobbyists develop judgment through extended play before deploying. Vibe coding deploys directly to production, producing what the article calls **crapjects** (outputs that prove a capability exists but serve no purpose) and **evaluative anesthesia** (the inability to distinguish valuable output from productivity theater when production is fast and cheap). Value accumulates upstream at the infrastructure / platform layer rather than with the makers themselves — a pattern the article calls **signal capture before upstream absorption**.

## Why it matters for the CMM
Three direct contributions to the v0.5 archetype set:

1. **Pioneer (Vanguard) risk profile.** "Crapjects" + "evaluative anesthesia" are the failure modes of an org that ships fast without governance — exactly the Vanguard's reversal-risk profile. The vibe-coding article supplies the vocabulary the litepaper and assessment use to describe this.
2. **Architect / Platform Builder moat.** "Signal capture before upstream absorption" is the structural moat the Architect builds — every rapid iterator's failed prototype feeds the platform's improvement, and the platform layer captures the value the makers themselves don't.
3. **Guild — tasteful discrimination.** The article's framing of *judgment* as the pre-vibe-coding capability that practitioners used to develop is the source of the "tasteful discrimination" concept now in the Guild archetype's profile — pattern recognition about what works that base models don't possess and vendors can't replicate.

## Claims citable from this observation
- Vibe coding skipped the scenius phase of judgment development that earlier maker movements went through.
- "Crapjects" — outputs that prove capability exists but serve no purpose.
- "Evaluative anesthesia" — inability to judge output quality when production is fast and cheap.
- Signal capture happens at the infrastructure / platform layer rather than with the makers.
