# Four Paradigms of CRFT (Nation of Artisans)

## Summary
Names four paradigms organizations operate within when they engage with AI-as-craft: paradigms differ on whether AI augments existing operations or opens new frontiers, and on the locus of judgment (institutional vs. individual). The article uses the framing "load-bearing" / "condition of possibility" to distinguish AI that supports existing capability from AI that *enables* a capability that wouldn't exist without it.

## Why it matters for the CMM
Direct contribution to the archetype taxonomy in v0.4:

1. **Augmentation vs. Architecture (Stake axis).** The CRFT paradigms cleanly distinguish "AI augments existing operations" from "AI enables a frontier that wouldn't otherwise exist." This is the `Stake` axis in the assessment's hierarchical scoring — Augmentation archetypes (Integrator / Tinkerer / Warden / Broker) sit on AI as capability layer; Architecture archetypes (Vanguard / Traveller / Architect / Ratifier) sit on AI as foundation.
2. **"Load-bearing" / "condition of possibility" vocabulary.** The Traveller archetype profile uses this exact framing in its core strategic bet — AI as the condition of possibility for the business, not augmentation of an existing business. Vocabulary lifted from this article.
3. **Cross-check on archetype set.** The Four Paradigms framing was used during v0.2 as an external validity check on the Augmentation/Frontiers split — confirms the distinction is recognised outside Protocolized's framing.

## Claims citable from this observation
- AI engagement falls into four paradigms differentiated by (a) whether AI augments existing capability or creates new frontiers, and (b) where judgment lives (institutional vs. individual).
- "Load-bearing" / "condition of possibility" as a way to distinguish AI as foundation vs. AI as capability layer.
