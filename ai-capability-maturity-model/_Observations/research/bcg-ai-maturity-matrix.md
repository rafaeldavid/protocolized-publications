# BCG — AI Maturity Matrix (November 2024)

## Summary
Boston Consulting Group's national-level AI maturity assessment, mapping 73 economies along readiness and exposure axes. Only 5 of 73 economies qualify as **AI Pioneers**. BCG names a distinct archetype — **Exposed Practitioners** — for economies with high-exposure sectors but low readiness. Cites Goldman Sachs (May 2024) projection of $632B in global AI spending by 2028 and reports that AI use boosted some companies' revenues by 2.5x versus competitors.

## Why it matters for the CMM
Section 1 of the litepaper uses this as the contrast source: BCG operates at the *national* level, which clarifies why an *organizational* model is needed. Three contributions:

1. **Scale contrast.** BCG measures countries; the CMM measures firms. Naming the difference clarifies what existing maturity work does and doesn't address — an enterprise sitting in an AI Pioneer economy can still be at Level 1.
2. **"Exposed Practitioners" archetype as a vocabulary anchor.** The exposure-without-readiness combination is the macro version of Level 1 / Level 2 organizations: high external pressure to deploy AI, low internal capacity to govern it.
3. **5-of-73 Pioneer ratio.** Useful sanity check on the "frontier-vs-median gap" thesis at national scale — the same long-tail distribution recurs across firms (Accenture's 12%) and economies (BCG's ~7%).

## Claims citable from this observation
- Only 5 of 73 economies qualify as AI Pioneers
- AI use boosted some companies' revenues by 2.5x vs. competitors
- Global AI spending projection: $632B by 2028 (Goldman Sachs, May 2024 — secondary citation in BCG)
- "Exposed Practitioners. This group includes developing and developed economies vulnerable to AI disruption due to more high-exposure sectors and low readiness." (verbatim, BCG)
