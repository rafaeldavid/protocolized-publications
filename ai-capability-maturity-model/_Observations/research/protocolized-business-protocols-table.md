# Protocolized — Table: Learning to See Business Protocols (2026)

## Summary
A reference table from Protocolized that catalogs business-protocol patterns and articulates a load-bearing observation: **protocols do not eliminate problems — they trade one class of problem for another.** Companion to the Atomic Protocol Questions work in the Theorizing Protocolization II piece. Functions as a working catalog of recognised business-protocol patterns and the trade-offs each carries, framed at a level of generality usable across industries and organizational types.

## Why it matters for the CMM
Direct precedent for the level-by-level failure-mode logic in Section 3 of the litepaper. Three contributions:

1. **Protocol trade pattern is the structural backbone of the level pages.** Each CMM level page identifies a primary problem the level *solves* and the new class of problem it *introduces*. That structure is lifted from this table's framing.
2. **Justifies the "no terminal level" reading of the model.** If every protocol trades problems rather than eliminating them, then no level is a destination — Level 5 has its own characteristic failure mode (planet-scale fragility). This is consistent with how the litepaper treats Stinson-Schroff's Mechanical Currents.
3. **Vocabulary alignment with Atomic Protocol Questions.** The table sits alongside Theorizing Protocolization II as a companion piece — both draw from the same question framework about what a protocol stabilizes, what it suppresses, and what it makes legible.
