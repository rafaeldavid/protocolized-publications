The Maturity Pyramid — a path that becomes itself again.

The same five stages, drawn as a single path. Cow-track, marked trail, stone way, paved road, and finally a road that loosens its grip and is reabsorbed by the world it once cut through. The pyramid is the icon. The path is what fills it.

L1 · BASE
Cow-path — desire lines.
PATHorganic, multi-track

The path is not yet a path. Many feet, many wanders — whichever route gets there. The ground records the strongest tendencies, no more.
In AI terms: shadow adoption. People route around official tools because something else got them there faster.

Origin · Pre-protocol
L2 · LOWER STRATUM
Marked trail — signposts and stones.
PATHbeaten, signed

One track wins. It gets named, marked, and walked again and again. The ground compacts; the route is now a route.
In AI terms: sanctioned use — an approved tool list, lightweight rules, but no architecture yet.

Permission · Wayfinding
L3 · MIDDLE STRATUM
Stone way — deliberate paving.
PATHlaid stone, engineered

Slabs are cut, fitted, set. The path is now a thing that took planning. It survives weather, traffic, and the absence of any single walker.
In AI terms: designed protocols. Review boards, evaluation gates, durable artifacts that outlast the team that wrote them.

Designed · Engineered
L4 · UPPER STRATUM
Paved road — infrastructural.
PATHasphalt, lit, signed

The road is no longer a path; it is infrastructure. Lit at night, signed for any stranger, carrying things the original walkers could not.
In AI terms: infrastructural AI — protocols become the substrate that other systems build on.

Infrastructural · Substrate
L5 · APEX
Reabsorption — road becomes ground.
PATHfragmenting, overgrown

The road lets go. Grass grows through. Fragments remain, but the boundary between road and world has dissolved — the route is part of the terrain now.
In AI terms: the protocol becomes ambient. Compliance shifts to planetary norms; the firm's structure fades into the field.

Planetary · Ambient

How to read

Read upward: the same path is shown at five points in its own life, stacked into the pyramid silhouette.

Read through time: a single walker, a generation later a stone-mason, a generation after that a road-crew — and finally the world, taking it back.

Why a path

A path is the simplest protocol. It records past coordination — who went here, in what direction — and constrains future coordination without enforcing it.

Stratifying the path through the pyramid keeps the icon's mnemonic shape while the metaphor underneath is one any walker understands without explanation.

Where the metaphor cuts

A real path gets harder, not softer, as it matures. Here the most mature stage looks the most fragile — that's the point. The protocol's job is done when it can disappear into the world it served.

Mnemonic glyph · printable

Triangle on a dim horizon-line, with the apex disintegrating upward. The path runs across the bottom — a soft umber stroke, more felt than seen at this scale. Vertex circles (substack motif) anchor the base. Suitable for favicons, slide corners, and the assessment-result chip on the site.
