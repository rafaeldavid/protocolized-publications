The Maturity Pyramid — an inverted watershed.

Five stages of organizational AI maturity, drawn as the human management of water. Springs at the base. Ocean at the apex. The pyramid is the structure; the water is the flow that the structure shapes — and ultimately releases.

L5 · APEX

In AI terms: the protocol becomes ambient. Compliance shifts to planetary norms; the firm's edge fades into the field.

Planetary · Ambient

How to read

Read upward: each stratum requires the one below to function. A dam without springs is dry; a generator without a dam idles.

Read outward: the apex is not a peak achieved — it is where the firm's structure fades into a field larger than itself.

Why a watershed

A pyramid alone implies climbing. A watershed implies flow. Inverting the watershed lets the pyramid keep its mnemonic shape while the metaphor underneath says: you are managing a moving substance, not stacking stones.

Where the metaphor cuts

A real watershed flows down. This one rises. The inversion is intentional: maturity is not gravity-fed. It takes work to lift water from springs to apex — and the apex is precisely where the work stops mattering, because the system has joined a larger one.

Mnemonic glyph · printable

Reduces to a triangle whose apex disintegrates upward: the canonical pyramid silhouette with a vapor crown.
