How to read

Read left to right: a single road across a single landscape, drawn at five points in its own life.

The pyramid is the reading frame overlaid on the picture. Look through it to know which stage the road is at; look past it to see the road itself.

Why a path

A path is the simplest protocol. It records past coordination — who went here, in what direction — and constrains future coordination without enforcing it.

Letting the path evolve along one continuous line keeps the metaphor doing the work. The pyramid only marks where you are along it.

Where the metaphor cuts

A real road gets harder, not softer, as it matures. Here the most mature stage looks the most fragile.

That is the point: the protocol's job is done when it can disappear into the world it served.
