Chapter 2 · Adoption Maturity Levels

AI tools entered through individual initiative rather than organizational decision. Employees use personal accounts for work tasks, no policy governs what data leaves the organization, and exposure is invisible until a leak, a regulatory inquiry, or a quality failure surfaces it.

Blind spot

No coordination mechanism specifies what tools may be used, what data may be submitted, what outputs require review, or who is accountable. The organization cannot distinguish reckless personal use from sophisticated bilateral pipelines; both are invisible.

Failure mode

Unaccountable AI access. AI systems operate with organizational data and produce outputs entering organizational decisions without any protocol governing accountability.

Anchor case

April 2023: three Samsung engineers submitted proprietary source code to ChatGPT through personal accounts within one month — discovered after the fact, banned enterprise-wide. Reco.ai (2025): 71% of enterprise employees use unauthorized AI tools, with a 400-day average discovery lag.

Historical parallel · late 1990s

Personal Hotmail and Yahoo accounts for business communication before corporate email was ubiquitous. Resolved through IT-provisioned corporate email with governance.
